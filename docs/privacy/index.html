Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Nuvio RS Plus is a personal Android TV media application. This policy explains how the app handles Google account and Google Drive data when the optional Google sync feature is used.

Google account information

When you choose to connect a Google account, Google OAuth is used to authenticate you. The app may receive basic account information necessary to identify the connected account, such as the account email address.

Google Drive access

Nuvio RS Plus requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. This scope is limited to files created by the app or files that the user explicitly opens with the app. The app does not request unrestricted access to all files in the user's Google Drive.

Google Drive access is used only to store and synchronize Nuvio RS Plus application data for the connected user.

Data sharing and sale

Nuvio RS Plus does not sell Google user data. Google account and Google Drive data accessed by the app are not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Data storage

Authentication tokens and application settings needed for the optional sync feature are stored by the application on the user's device. Synced application data is stored in the user's own Google Drive through the permissions granted by the user.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Google sync from within Nuvio RS Plus. You can also revoke the app's access from your Google Account permissions at any time.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be submitted through the project's GitHub repository.
